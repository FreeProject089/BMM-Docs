Pick the job you came to do. Each page is one task: short steps, what you should see after each
one, a picture, and a button that shows you the same thing in BMM itself.

## How these pages work

- [ ] **Tick the steps as you go.** The little boxes remember where you stopped, on this PC only. **Start over** clears them.
- [ ] **Press a Show me button.** In BMM it opens the screen or starts the matching interactive tutorial, so you can follow along on the real app. On the website it is only a label.
- [ ] **Press a picture to play it.** The short clips are still until you press them, so a page never moves by itself. Press again to pause.

:::tip[Every step says what you should see]
Steps end with *You see:*. If your screen does not match, stop there and read the
**If it does not work** list at the bottom of the page before going further.
:::
